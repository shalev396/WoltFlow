WoltFlow

📋 Application Purpose

WoltFlow is an automation application that automatically purchases Wolt gift cards from Cibus on a daily basis.

The application saves users time by eliminating manual gift card purchases and ensures maximum utilization of meal benefits through intelligent automation scheduling.

🔐 Why WoltFlow Needs Your Google Account

  • Gmail API Access: Automatically retrieve 2FA verification codes sent to your email during the automated purchase process
  • Account Authentication: Secure login to your WoltFlow dashboard without storing additional passwords
  • Notification Delivery: Send you real-time updates about successful purchases, errors, or required actions
  • Data Security: We only read specific emails related to purchase verification - your personal emails remain private

Powered by Selenium Automation

WoltFlow utilizes Selenium WebDriver technology to automate the gift card purchase process:

  • Automated browser control for seamless purchases
  • Smart error handling and retry mechanisms
  • Screenshot capture for transaction verification
  • Real-time status monitoring and notifications
  • Gmail integration for secure 2FA code retrieval
